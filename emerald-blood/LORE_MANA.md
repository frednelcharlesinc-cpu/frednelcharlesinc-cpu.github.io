**OWNER REVISION (2026-11-17):**
- **No ongoing conditions.** No oaths that crack, no geas that burn runes out, no rules you must keep to *use* an art. Once you have it, it's yours.
- **Instead, GETTING these arts is brutally hard.** Hidden masters, near-death trials, steep requirements, real failure. Most people in the world never learn any of them; most who try fail.
- **The South's Way is BEAST-BOND** (owner picked it; the drum/song idea was rejected).


**OWNER RULE (2026-11-17, overrides any wording below): the Ways are mostly PASSIVE.** Ironing (coating), Verdance (sage power)
and rune weapons are **add-ons that permanently improve a character's stats and abilities** — not moves, not actions that spend turns.
**Example: once Ren unlocks coating, he coats his blade AUTOMATICALLY as every fight begins** (a short visual at battle start) — it is never a menu move.
**The exception is ONYX'S CLOAK: that is an active change** — Ren transforms into it during a fight (like a power-up moment), and it ends.
Learn a tier → the bonus is simply always there. Read every art below through this rule:
| Art | Passive effect (A sets numbers) |
|---|---|
| **Ironing** (North) | Grey: hits ignore part of DEF · Black: weapon can't break + parries deal guard damage · Forward: a share of hits can't be dodged |
| **Hindsight** (North) | perfect-dodge/parry windows wider; enemy's next move shown in the turn bar |
| **Kingsweight** (North, master) | at the start of a fight, much weaker enemies may freeze, flee or surrender; enemy morale starts lower |
| **Rune tattoos** (East) | already passive: a permanent stat boost + one ability each (max 3) |
| **Rune weapons** (East) | can't be disarmed; thrown/recall follow-up hits; the weapon's own stat bonus grows with its name |
| **Verdance** (West) | Greenmind becomes a permanent bonus: +regeneration each turn, +DEF/+ATK, longer reach; Rootstep = a % chance to auto-dodge |
| **Kin / Beast-Bond** (South) | the beast fights beside you on its own (companion); Borrow = permanent traits from the beast (armour, speed, sight) |
| **Onyx's Cloaks — ACTIVE (the exception)** | Ren CHANGES into it mid-fight (a transformation moment, lasts a few turns). Night Mantle: +SPD, wider perfect windows, see hidden enemies · Starless Cloak: + Onyx's breath follow-ups and fire resistance |
ACTIVE: the universal tiers — **Aura Force** (chosen), **Kaigen** (it happens), **Transcension** (story stages) — plus **Onyx's Cloak** (Ren changes into it) and Kinform as the South's rare ultimate.
Visual (Chat C): passives show as small constant touches (a dark sheen on an Ironed blade, leaf-light in Verdance eyes, a faint blue mantle edge for the Cloak, rune glow on the portrait) so players can SEE what a character has learned.

## The core idea
All four are **the same mana**. Each culture found a different way to master it, with its own name for it and its own view
of the Drags (which keeps the arts tied to the game's heart: discrimination against the green-eyed).

| | NORTH | EAST | WEST | SOUTH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| People | Vikings, jarls, war-clans | rune-smiths, ink-monks, tattooed legions | elves | desert tribes, reef-islanders, beast-riders |
| They call mana | **WILL** ("mana is for weaklings — this is *Will*") | **INK** (mana that has been *written*) | **VERDANCE** (the world's breath) | **KIN** (the thread every living thing shares) |
| Source | the self | marks on skin and steel | the land, through stillness | a bond with a beast |
| On Drags | "Can't Hindsight a Drag. Unnatural." | runes refuse Drag blood (the ink burns green) | elves remember the Drags before the lies | beasts smell the dragon in Drag blood and flee |
| Their word for KAIGEN | the Quiet Fury | the Open Rune | Stillwater | the Hunter's Hush |

The **Drags** use none of these normally: their mana is in their **blood** (the green; Drag Arts). Ren learning a piece of each Way, by
finding a way around each culture's barrier, is the shonen spine of the journey. **No one has ever mastered every Way.**

Universal tiers sit above the cultures: **Aura Force** → **KAIGEN** (can't be called) → **Transcension** (behind Kaigen's Second Door).

## How hard it is to learn — the shared ladder (use for every Way)
Each Way has **3 tiers** (Novice → Veteran → Master). Every tier needs ALL of:
1. **A teacher you have to find.** Masters hide, refuse students, or are dead; their students are scattered. Finding one is a quest chain (rumours, maps, side-bosses).
2. **A level and stat wall.** Novice ~Lv 20, Veteran ~Lv 35, Master ~Lv 50 (A sets the numbers). No shortcuts.
3. **A TRIAL that can be failed.** A hard scripted fight or ordeal, beaten under strict rules (no items, solo, a time limit, perfects only).
   Failing costs real things: an **Injury** (−stats for a number of in-game days), the teacher's patience (a cooldown before you can retry),
   and gold or materials for the next attempt.
4. **Rare materials** (Eastern ink, Northern frost-iron, elder sap…) found in deep zones, raids and boss drops.
5. **Time.** Training happens over in-game days at the teacher's camp; the party member is away (can't fight) while training.
Master tier also needs **beating the teacher in a duel**, and most teachers only ever accept one student who reaches it.

## NORTH — WILL
**Philosophy:** strength forced into being. Northerners train Will the hard way — cold, hunger, holding a line until the body breaks and the Will doesn't.

**How you get it:** a Northern war-master (a jarl's champion, or an exiled one) has to accept you. The trials:
- *Novice — the Cold Watch:* survive a night-long battle in a blizzard (a long fight where everyone slowly loses HP each turn) without retreating.
- *Veteran — the Iron Line:* hold a choke point against waves alone; one perfect parry missed resets the wave count.
- *Master — the Jarl's Duel:* beat your teacher, who uses all three arts against you.

**The three Will arts**
1. **IRONING** (coating Will onto a weapon or body)
   - *Grey Iron* (novice): the edge goes dark; hits ignore 50% DEF.
   - *Black Iron* (veteran): the weapon can't break; a coated PARRY deals guard damage to the attacker.
   - *Forward Iron* (master): Will is coated into the air *ahead* of the swing — the blow lands a heartbeat before the blade; cannot be dodged.
   - *Fist-iron*: unarmed fighters coat their skin (Liora's kung-fu could learn this).
2. **HINDSIGHT** — they don't see the future; they *remember being hit* before it happens, as if they've already lost this fight once.
   - Battle: the enemy's next skill and target are shown; perfect-dodge/parry windows +40%.
   - Master: "Long Hindsight" — remembers two moves ahead.
   - Drags' perfect body control blurs in Hindsight (the lore reason the North fears them). Fighters in KAIGEN can't be Hindsighted ("they hadn't decided yet").
3. **KINGSWEIGHT** — the crushing presence. Master tier only, and rare even among masters.
   - Once per fight: weaker enemies lose their turn, drop their weapons or flee; enemy morale crashes (the morale/surrender systems already exist).
   - Two Kingsweights clashing: the sky darkens, the ground splits — who blinks first.
   - Ren's version later: a *green* Kingsweight — the Drags' own.

**People:** jarl-champions, war-skalds, **Grimtusk** (an Uncrowned — tore a drawbridge off its chains with Will-ironed hands).

## EAST — INK (runes on skin and steel)
**Philosophy:** mana is wild until it is *written*. Rune-smiths and ink-monks write mana into skin and steel; written mana never fades.

**How you get a rune tattoo:** this is the hardest Way to enter.
- Reach a rune-temple (hidden; the way there is a quest chain).
- **Pass the temple's trial for that rune** — each rune has its own ordeal, e.g. the Rune of Return's trial is a fight you must survive
  for 10 turns at 1 HP; the Rune of the Breaker's is breaking a guard no one has broken.
- **Survive the inking.** The tattooing itself is a fight against the ink's mana (a scripted battle where the rune attacks *you*).
  Lose and the ink fades — gold, materials and a long cooldown gone.
- Rare Eastern ink (deep zones / raid drops) for every rune. Max **3** runes per body; the skin can't hold a fourth.

**Example runes** (permanent stat boost + one ability, yours forever once taken):
- *Return* — +SPD; once per battle survive a lethal hit at 1 HP.
- *Ember* — +ATK; hits burn. (Kairo earns this one — a rivalry beat.)
- *Unbent* — +DEF; immune to fear and Kingsweight.
- *Long Road* — +SPD; faster travel.
- *Ash-Sight* — see enemy HP and weak points.
- *Breaker* — Break gauge ×1.5.
- *Silence* — enemies can't read or predict you.

**Rune weapons are NAMED.** A weapon is named by a rune-smith (rare, expensive, months of in-game time) after the wielder proves
themselves to the smith in a duel. Speak the name → it RETURNS to the hand from anywhere (throw-and-recall attacks, can't be disarmed).
Story: some old named weapons choose who may lift them.

**Drags and Ink:** runes refuse Drag blood — the ink burns green and slides off. Eastern masters call it proof of the curse.
Then **one rune takes on a Drag** (Ren) — a rune no one has seen in 400 years. Big East-arc beat.

**People:** ink-monks, rune-legions, smith-clans; **King Ash** (an Uncrowned).

## WEST — VERDANCE (the elves)
**Philosophy:** the world breathes mana; be still and breathe with it.

**How you get it:** elves almost never teach outsiders. You need an elder's sponsorship (a long quest earning the trust of an elven grove), then:
- *Novice — the Long Stillness:* a battle where you may not act at all for several turns while enemies attack; any action fails the trial.
- *Veteran — the Rootless Walk:* cross a living forest that drinks mana from anyone who isn't perfectly calm (a maze where every misstep costs MP).
- *Master — Taking Root:* breathe in more Verdance than your body can hold and come back out. Masters show bark-scars from this trial for the rest of their lives; those who fail become trees. (The great trees of the elven woods **are** elves who failed — still alive, still listening.)

**Arts**
- **Gathering** — spend turns perfectly still to breathe Verdance in; each turn stacks power.
- **Greenmind** (empowered state) — +ATK/+DEF/+regeneration, attacks reach through the air itself, a **ring of leaf-light** in the eyes.
- **Rootstep** (veteran) — teleport-dodge to anywhere you've stood this battle.
- **Drinking a Dominion** (master) — breathe in a monster's Dominion field and turn its power against it.

**Already half in the game:** Vane's **Tomb Breath** (`G.S.f.cultivation`) is a Western technique → Vane once studied with the elves.
**Drags and Verdance:** the oldest elves *remember the Drags before the lies* — the West is where Ren learns the truth, and where the elves refuse to say it in public.

## SOUTH — KIN (Beast-Bond)
**Philosophy:** every living thing shares one thread of mana, KIN. Southern fighters don't push mana out of themselves; they tie their
thread to a beast's, and the two become one fighter. Desert tribes bond sand-wyrms, dune-cats and great hawks; reef-islanders bond
sharks, rays and sea-serpents.

**How you get it:** you can't buy, tame or capture a bond. **The beast decides.**
- *Novice — the First Bond:* find a wild beast in its own territory (rare spawns in Southern zones), follow it, survive it, and beat it
  **without killing it** (a scripted fight where reducing it to 0 HP fails the trial; you have to Break it and hold it there). Only then
  does it accept your thread. Fail and that beast is gone for good; you have to find another.
- *Veteran — the Shared Hunt:* hunt alongside your beast through a dangerous zone; the beast fights its own way and you have to read it
  (a battle where you can't command it, only follow its lead).
- *Master — the Great Beast:* bond one of the continent's legendary beasts (a raid-tier fight in its lair; each Great Beast accepts only one bond in a lifetime).
Southern masters are few; most bonded fighters stay at Novice their whole lives.

**The three Kin arts**
1. **BOND** (novice) — your beast fights beside you as a second fighter (its own turns, its own moves). It can be knocked out; it comes back after the battle.
2. **BORROW** (veteran) — take on your beast's nature for a few turns: a sand-wyrm's armour (huge DEF), a reef shark's speed and bleed, a hawk's sight (see enemy weaknesses, never miss), a dune-cat's reflexes (dodge window ×2).
3. **KINFORM** (master) — fighter and beast become one for a few turns: the beast's shape over your body (wings, armour, fins, claws). Not a Transcension — the body is borrowed, not changed — but it's the South's ultimate.

**Drags and Kin:** beasts smell the dragon in Drag blood and flee; no beast has ever bonded with a Drag. Except one: **Onyx**, Ren's little dragon,
already IS his bond. The South arc reveals what that means — and why it can change shape (into a cat, or anything): a bond-beast that shapeshifts is
something no Southern master has ever heard of. (Onyx's power stops at the CLOAK — see below. Dragonification is the Drags' own.)

**People:** desert beast-riders, reef raiders, **Mother Brine** (an Uncrowned — her bond is a sea-serpent the size of a warship; that's how she sank three Imperial ships in one night).
**Their word for Kaigen:** *the Hunter's Hush* — "when you and the beast breathe as one and the prey hasn't heard you yet."

## How Ren (and the party) touches each Way — the shonen spine
- **North:** a Northern master refuses him (can't Hindsight a Drag, "unnatural") — Ren earns the Cold Watch anyway; Grey Iron on his katana.
- **East:** every rune refuses him… until the 400-year rune takes. Kairo gets Ember the normal way.
- **West:** the elves teach him Stillness → it deepens his Still Sky Transcension; he learns the truth about the Drags.
- **South:** no beast will bond with a Drag — then the South learns Onyx is a bond-beast that shapeshifts. Ren and Onyx reach the Cloak.
- **Endgame:** a Drag who has touched every Way — the thing the Monarchs fear most.

## THE GREAT BEASTS & BEARERS (owner-approved, Naruto/Kurama-inspired, made our own — 2026-11-17)
**The Great Beasts:** **seven** ancient beasts, each a shard of the old world's mana from before the Drags and Fratenaptor. Each has a name,
a personality and a will of its own, and each can be bonded (Kin) by exactly one person in its lifetime.
- Where they are: some wild (raid-tier lairs), **one bonded to Mother Brine** (her sea-serpent), **one chained under the Crown's capital** as a weapon,
  the rest scattered — A/B decide.
- **The Monarchs are hunting all seven** (our version of the Akatsuki plot) — a reason for the whole world to fight over them.
- **Bearers:** anyone bonded to a Great Beast is a *Bearer* — feared and shunned everywhere, like the Drags. Bearers and Drags are natural allies;
  Mother Brine is the first adult who truly understands Ren. Each of the other Great Beasts has a Bearer the party meets: rival, ally or tragedy —
  each one a mini-boss arc.

**ONYX, Ren's familiar — a character, not a pet** (already in the game: companions.js, black as a starless night, huge blue eyes, blue dragonfire,
hatched from an egg in a ruined Drag shrine; the player can rename it; Hatchling → Whelp → Young Dragon as Ren ranks up)
- **Personality (NOT a grumpy fox):** cheeky, curious, greedy for food, sleeps on Ren's head — and every so often an ancient memory leaks out of it in an
  old, old voice, then it's a baby again and doesn't remember saying it.
- **Talks to Ren in his head:** short lines during scenes and battles; opinions on NPCs; sometimes refuses to help.
- **Jobs outside battle:** scouts disguised as a cat (or other small animals as it grows), sniffs out hidden chests/caches, reads a stranger's intent
  (hostile/friendly hint before a talk), carries small things through gaps and locked bars.
- **Its trust is earned through story, not grinding:**
  1. at first it doesn't fully trust Ren;
  2. a turning point — Onyx saves Ren on its own;
  3. a real conversation where Ren learns what Onyx is (what is a dragon doing in a Drag shrine?);
  4. they fight as equals.
- **THE CLOAK — the furthest Onyx's power goes** (owner: Onyx's bond stops at the cloak):
  - **Cloak I — Night Mantle:** a thin mantle of blue-black dragonfire over Ren; his eyes get a blue ring; he borrows Onyx's senses and speed
    (see hidden enemies, +SPD, perfect windows wider).
  - **Cloak II — Starless Cloak:** a full shroud of Onyx's fire wrapped around Ren — wings of dark blue flame from his back, a dragon-head hood of flame,
    flame claws on his hands. Onyx fights *through* him (it takes some of Ren's turns with its own breath attacks). Ren's BODY does not change.
- **DRAGONIFICATION is NOT Onyx's.** Ren's Stage III Dragonoid is a pure Drag ability — the dragon in Drag blood waking — and needs no familiar.
  Any Drag strong enough could, in theory, reach it (huge story hook: the Dread General is a Drag…).
- **Looks must never blur:** Onyx's Cloak = BLUE and BLACK fire around an unchanged body. Ren's Transcension = GREEN/SILVER, his own body changing.
  Kaigen = colour drained, lightning in the eyes. Aura Force = flames in the fighter's own colour.
