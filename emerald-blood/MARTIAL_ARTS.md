# Martial arts (proposal from Chat C, owner's request 2026-11-12)

Every art builds on the four weapon arts in `js/fourcuts.js` (Four Cuts · Four Lines · Four Strikes · Four Shadows).
Chat A owns lore: rename or rewrite freely. Sprites follow whatever lands here. Only Ren (and Vane / the Emperor's Champion, same art) draws from a sheath.

Weapon decisions made for consistency: **Oren = forge hammer** (fights the Four Strikes with it: 'a hammer is just a heavier fist'); **Tamsin = torch-bow** (a recurve bow with bladed limbs she sets alight — the 'torch-blade'); **Cael = halberd** (matches lore.js Drag-Breaker).

## Ren — The Sheathed Draw (Vane's style)
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a katana with a plain dark guard and a dark red-brown lacquered scabbard worn at the left hip*

Taught for five years in the Realm by Vane, the one-armed Sword Saint, whose art it is. Vane spent twenty years building it after Dread took his arm and tore the stone from his sword: an art for a man who cannot afford a second cut. Its law is Vane's: 'A sword is loudest in its sheath. The draw IS the cut.' Ren is the only living student - which is why everyone who sees it asks the same question: who taught you that?

- Stance: low and still, weight forward, left hand on the scabbard, right hand resting on the hilt, blade fully sheathed
- Opener: DRAW - 7 frames: hand on hilt, the body coils, an explosive draw-cut that leaves a green crescent, the follow-through, the blade sliding back into the sheath with a click
- Combo: three lightning draw-cuts (Rise, Fall, Wind), each one snapping back into the scabbard between strikes
- Signature: Horizon Draw - one draw-cut across the whole enemy line, a long green horizon-line of light hanging in the air after the blade is home
- Guard: half-draws the blade to catch the hit on the steel, then strikes with the scabbard · Dodge: a single silent sidestep, leaving a fading afterimage

## Kess — Shadowstep Knife-Dance
*Base: Four Shadows · Weapon: two curved daggers held in reverse grip*

Kess learned the Four Shadows the way street kids do - by watching the people who robbed her and doing it faster. The 'dance' part is her own: she never stands still long enough to be hit, and she fights from inside her opponent's shadow. She taught Ren how to fall without breaking, and she has never once let him win.

- Stance: crouched low, both daggers reversed along the forearms, swaying side to side
- Opener: SHADOW FLICK - 7 frames: she drops into her own shadow and rises behind the target with a reverse-grip cut
- Combo: Flick, Fan, Sting: two spinning slashes with violet trails, then a stab
- Signature: Shadowfang - her violet shadow peels off the ground and strikes with her: five cuts from two directions
- Guard: crosses both daggers in an X to catch the blow · Dodge: melts into her shadow and pops up a step away

## Liora — Moonwater Spear
*Base: Four Lines · Weapon: a long spear with a slim leaf-shaped blade and a red tassel*

Her mother's school: the Four Lines taught like water - never block what you can let flow past. Her mother also told her that only one swordsman alive fought with his blade sheathed until the cut, and that he was supposed to be dead - which is why Liora recognised Ren's style the moment she saw it. 'Moonwater' was her mother's name for it; the moonlight part is Liora's own.

- Stance: spear held low and level, weight on the back foot, calm
- Opener: MOONSTEP - 7 frames: a flowing pole-vault over the enemy, landing in a spiral thrust
- Combo: Sweep, Brace, Lunge: a low sweeping arc, a spinning butt-strike, then a long straight thrust
- Signature: Wyrm Thrust - a spiralling lunge as a pale serpent of light coils down the spear
- Guard: spins the spear like a wheel in front of her · Dodge: vaults on the spear shaft over the attack

## Oren — Anvil Breaker
*Base: Four Strikes · Weapon: a heavy two-handed forge hammer*

Oren fights the Four Strikes with a hammer instead of a fist: 'a hammer is just a heavier fist.' Every swing is a forging stroke - lift, fall, strike true - eleven years of shaping iron turned on people. The forge-spirit that has slept in his embers for those eleven years answers when he swings hard enough.

- Stance: wide and planted, hammer resting on his shoulder
- Opener: FORGE SWING - 7 frames: a full-body overhead hammer swing that hits the ground in a burst of embers
- Combo: Jab, Hook, Hammer: a short hammer-butt jab, a sideways hook swing, a crushing overhead
- Signature: Cleave - the hammer splits the ground and a wave of forge-fire runs out across every enemy
- Guard: takes the hit on his bracers and the hammer head with a burst of sparks · Dodge: a short heavy shoulder-roll

## Dace — Iron Vow Axe
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a double-bladed battle axe*

Green Eclipse's front line. Dace swore an iron vow never to step back from a fight while a comrade stands behind him, and he fights like it: the style has almost no retreat in it, only weight. He uses the Four Cuts with an axe because nobody ever taught him anything else, and nobody has ever needed to.

- Stance: axe on his shoulder, chin down, feet planted
- Opener: IRON VOW - 7 frames: he plants his feet, raises the axe two-handed and brings it down in one brutal chop
- Combo: Rise, Fall, Wind with an axe: an upward cut, a downward chop, a full spinning sweep
- Signature: Vowbreaker - an overhead chop that cracks the ground and sends a grey iron shockwave
- Guard: hooks the enemy weapon with the axe's beard and shoves it away · Dodge: a barely-there lean - he refuses to step back

## Tamsin — Wildfire Archery
*Base: Four Lines · Weapon: a recurve torch-bow whose limbs are bladed and can be set alight; fire arrows*

Tamsin's bow is also a blade: its limbs are edged steel, and when she lights them it becomes the torch-blade the stories talk about. She learned the Four Lines as an archer's footwork - every arrow is a spear thrown from far away - and added the fire herself.

- Stance: arrow nocked, half-drawn, bow held low
- Opener: WILDFIRE SHOT - 7 frames: draws, the arrowhead ignites, looses a burning arrow that leaves a fire trail
- Combo: a rapid triple-shot, then a slash with the burning blade-limb of the bow
- Signature: Firestorm Volley - she fires straight up and a rain of burning arrows falls
- Guard: knocks the hit aside with the bow's bladed limb · Dodge: a backflip, firing while upside down

## Kairo — Scar-Spark Blade
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a straight single-edged sword whose edge glows green*

Kairo and Ren have been keeping score since they were six. The night Thornvale burned, Kairo ran back into the fire and pulled eleven people out, and his body learned the fire: it stopped being allowed to hurt him. He left the burn on his face to scar so he would never forget. His sword art grew out of that night - the Four Cuts thrown at full strength, every one throwing sparks, and the more he is hurt the hotter his scars burn (Scar Fuel). He has never met a style he couldn't beat by trying harder. He has met one now.

- Stance: aggressive forward lean, sword held high and back
- Opener: SCAR-SPARK RUSH - 7 frames: a running slash that throws green sparks and ash
- Combo: wild Rise-Fall-Wind arcs trailing green fire
- Signature: Ashfall - an ash-and-ember spiral slash
- Guard: meets the blow blade-to-blade and shoves · Dodge: bursts sideways in a puff of ash-smoke

## Vane — The Sheathed Draw (original form)
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a katana with an empty stone-socket in its guard (its stone was torn out)*

The Sword Saint. Forty years ago Dread took his arm and tore the stone from his sword. A one-armed man without a stone cannot win a long fight, so Vane spent twenty years teaching himself to win the first exchange: never draw until the cut, never leave the blade out after it. 'A sword is loudest in its sheath.' The world thinks he is dead. He trained exactly one student.

- Stance: perfectly still, one hand on the hilt, eyes half closed
- Opener: DRAW - 7 frames: stillness, an invisible draw, the cut appears, the blade is already home
- Combo: three draw-cuts so small you barely see the blade
- Signature: First Snow - the air freezes and snow falls after one cut
- Guard: deflects with the scabbard without drawing · Dodge: a half-step

## The Emperor's Champion — The Unbowed Draw
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a katana whose guard holds a wind-stone (Gale Sovereign)*

Vane's draw, stolen. The Champion watched the Sword Saint fight once and spent years rebuilding the art from memory, then married it to the Gale Sovereign stone. The cut lands before the wind it makes, and it cannot be parried by anyone who has not seen it before. Ren has seen it before.

- Stance: a mirror of Vane's stance - but proud and upright
- Opener: UNBOWED DRAW - 7 frames: a draw-cut that lands before the gust of wind it makes
- Combo: three draw-cuts riding gusts of wind
- Signature: Gale Sovereign - a draw that releases a blade of wind across the field
- Guard: a sheath-parry that turns into a counter-draw · Dodge: rides a gust sideways

## Inquisitor Halvard — Pale Liturgy
*Base: Four Lines · Weapon: a tall staff topped with a pale Verdict-stone, and a holy tome chained at his hip*

The staff forms of the Four Lines, taught in the Inquisition as prayer. Every strike is a line of liturgy, and the Verdict-stone on his staff was cut to burn Drags: it makes their arts hit harder - and it judges the one who uses them.

- Stance: upright, staff planted, tome floating open beside him
- Opener: VERDICT - 7 frames: he reads a line from the tome and strikes with the staff in a flash of white fire
- Combo: staff strikes that leave white flame
- Signature: Pale Saint - white fire pours from his eyes and staff
- Guard: a ward of white light · Dodge: glides backwards without moving his feet

## Corran — The Rules
*Base: Four Cuts · Weapon: a plain longsword held two-handed*

Corran keeps Green Eclipse alive with a list of rules, and his sword works the same way: no wasted motion, no pride, no second chances given. Every Eclipse fighter learned their first cut from him. The Last Rule is the one he has never told anyone.

- Stance: textbook guard, sword held high, perfectly balanced
- Opener: FIRST RULE - 7 frames: a measured step and a single perfect cut
- Combo: three economical longsword cuts with no wasted motion
- Signature: The Last Rule - a blue-aura half-sword finisher
- Guard: binds the enemy blade and controls it · Dodge: one measured step back

## Captain Voss — Wall Doctrine
*Base: Four Lines · Weapon: a heater shield and an arming sword*

The city guard's doctrine, written by men who expected to hold a gate, not win a duel: never give ground, never leave the shield. Voss has drilled it until it is less a style than a wall that walks.

- Stance: shield first, sword low behind it
- Opener: SHIELD WALL - 7 frames: he advances behind the shield and stabs from behind it
- Combo: shield bash, short stab, shield bash
- Signature: Iron Warden - the shield slams down and the ground cracks
- Guard: shield punch · Dodge: sets his feet and braces instead of moving

## Ren's road through the Sheathed Draw (proposal — owner's request 2026-11-12; the Four Cuts were retired the same day)
Ren learns Vane's art in pieces as his grade rises. His basic Attack (2 timed strikes) already uses five different Sheathed Draw strikes (MOVES sheet rows A–E); the techniques below are the VANE sheet (`art_*` anims).

| Grade | Learns |
|---|---|
| E | Attack (5 strike variants), Horizon Draw |
| C | **Tiger's Descent** (new), Mirage Step |
| A | **Fourfold Draw** (new): sheathe while time slows, one draw, four slashes at once |
| S | **Thousand Needles** (new) |
| SS | **Sword Saint's Reply** (new) |
| SSS+ | **First Snow** — Vane's own art (new) |

Techniques (gameplay is Chat A's):
- **TIGER'S DESCENT** (grade C): leaps high with the blade sheathed, draws in mid-air and falls on the enemy in one diagonal cleaving cut that crosses their whole body, landing crouched as the blade clicks home
- **MIRAGE STEP** (grade C): vanishes into an afterimage, reappears behind the enemy and cuts through; the afterimage fades a beat later
- **HORIZON DRAW** (grade E): one draw-cut across the whole enemy line; a long green horizon-line hangs in the air after the blade is home
- **FOURFOLD DRAW** (grade A): sheathes completely while time slows, then one explosive draw and four slashes land on the target at once in a cross pattern
- **THOUSAND NEEDLES** (grade S): a blur of point-first thrusts straight from the sheath, dozens of thin green lines hitting at once
- **SWORD SAINT'S REPLY** (grade SS): stands perfectly still with eyes closed as the enemy strikes, then a single counter-draw and six slashes appear on the attacker at once
- **FIRST SNOW** (grade SSS): Vane's own art: total stillness, one invisible cut, the air freezes and snow falls; the enemy only notices the cut after the blade is home
